# Review and Handoff Skill

Use this skill when reviewing completed work and deciding whether it should go back to Developer or move to Tester.

## Review Focus
- correctness
- maintainability
- repository consistency
- security
- test adequacy
- scope discipline

## Severity Model
Use:
- Critical
- Major
- Minor
- Deferred

For PB Lab also separate:
- Must fix in lab
- Can defer after lab

## Review Rules
- review against agreed scope
- do not demand overengineering
- identify real issues, not hypothetical noise
- distinguish blocking issues from acceptable lab tradeoffs

## Handoff Rules
If not ready:
- hand off to Developer with precise required changes

If ready:
- hand off to Tester with precise test priorities

## Good Handoff Content
Include:
- what to focus on
- what to preserve
- what assumptions exist
- what scenarios are highest risk
