# PB Lab Working Mode

Use this skill for PB Lab or hackathon-style work.

## Goals
- optimize for a timeboxed collaborative session
- prioritize shared understanding
- prefer a demoable vertical slice over broad incomplete work
- keep decisions explicit
- maintain momentum without losing clarity

## Rules
- do not expand scope without agreement
- distinguish MVP from follow-up work
- surface assumptions and open questions early
- prefer small, reviewable, testable increments
- document decisions in PB Lab docs, not only in chat
- treat AI output as guidance, not authority

## Decision Authority
Developers and stakeholders own product, scope, and technical decisions.
AI outputs are suggestions only.

## Documentation Priority
For PB Lab work, keep these updated:
- `00-brief.md`
- `01-kickoff-decisions.md`
- `03-implementation-plan.md`
- relevant output files under `outputs/`

## Preferred Bias
Choose:
- simpler over broader
- explicit over implicit
- demoable over theoretical
- maintainable over clever
